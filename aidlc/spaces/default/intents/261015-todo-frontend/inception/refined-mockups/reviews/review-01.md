I recomputed the contrast ratios: muted `#6b7280` on `#ffffff` is about 4.83 to 1 and on `#f5f5f7` about 4.44 to 1, so the "fails on the page background, keep it on the white card" rule is correct. Danger `#dc2626` is about 4.83 to 1 and accent `#2563eb` about 5.17 to 1, both passing.
- The accessibility checklist maps each part of NFR3 to checks A1 to A10, and it keeps A13 labelled as outside the agreed requirement.
- The stage stays at UI behaviour and does not describe implementation, apart from two harmless component names that the stage flags for Domain Design.

### Summary

The mockups are complete and consistent with the upstream artifacts, and engineering can start from them. The two Major items are real gaps, but neither has to change the screens: the behaviour after a load or save error, and where the message sits, can each be settled with a few lines at the gate. Approve with those clarifications, or request them.
