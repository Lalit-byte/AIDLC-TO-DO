### Summary

The contracts are clear and consistent with Q1 to Q3 (hook ids, whole-list saves, version 1, owner approval), the Task shape, NFR5 and NFR8, and they do not promise phase 2 work beyond the shape and version. NOT-READY because three Major gaps remain in the whole-list-save design: the first save after a load failure overwrites damaged data against FR6.3, stale tabs can overwrite each other with no stated mitigation, and the interaction of a failed change with tab sync (and of toggle, edit and remove with a task another tab removed) is not defined. The first two are left as open questions even though they change the C3 function signatures.
