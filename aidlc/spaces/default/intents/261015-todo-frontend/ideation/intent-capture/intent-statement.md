# Intent Statement: Todo List Frontend

## Problem Statement

People need a way to capture tasks quickly and track what is done. [Q1]

The first of two phases builds the frontend of the todo list in basic React; the backend comes in phase 2. [desc]

## Target Customer

The intended users are the general public. [Q2]

## Success Metrics

- All frontend requirements in `PRD.md` (FR-1 to FR-7) pass their pass/fail checks. [Q3]
- Adding, toggling and deleting a task respond in under 100 ms. [Q3]

## Initiative Trigger

The project is being built for learning or practice. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `mvp`. [scope]
- User-confirmed product boundary for this first version: due dates and reminders, tags or categories, and drag-and-drop ordering are left out. [Q8]
- User accounts and login are not part of phase 1; each browser keeps its own tasks. [Q9]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

None.
