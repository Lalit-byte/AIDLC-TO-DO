The designs are consistent with the functional spec and cover every NFR. The two Major gaps are the unspecified, and on current React probably wrong, way the save is moved after the draw, and the missing test for NFR1.2. Both are fixable in the document. READY is within the verdict limit of two Majors, and both should be fixed before Code Generation.
