### Summary

The acceptance-criteria traceability is sound, but the notice state machine contradicts BR1.12, W4 and contract C4 and can hide save failures. The load/save shape check, the two-owner backup state, the unlisted contract deviations and the `crypto.randomUUID` dependency leave more than two Major gaps, so a developer would have to guess.
